# Results: Experiment 2, information latency

Run 20 October 2026. The pre-registration ([PREREGISTRATION.md](PREREGISTRATION.md),
commit 98a89f7) and the gate numbers ([GATE.md](GATE.md), commit 16ba5e3) were
both committed before any odds were read for this experiment. Every table quoted
here comes from two generated files:

- [report.md](report.md): `python -m nbatotals.experiments.info_latency`, built
  from the harness's own grading functions;
- [../../backtest_legacy_report_lineup_pace_eff.md](../../backtest_legacy_report_lineup_pace_eff.md):
  `python -m nbatotals backtest --legacy --model lineup_pace_eff`, in the format of
  the main pre-registered report.

Both need the maintainer's private data (env vars in
`nbatotals/evaluate/adapters/legacy_players.py`). The main pre-registered report
(`docs/backtest_legacy_report.md`) regenerated byte for byte after the harness
change made here (the Pinnacle table can now be graded at any anchor).

## In plain words

1. **The inputs are pre-game, not back-filled.** Dunks & Threes' projected
   minutes and its player ratings both pass the pre-registered gate. The ratings
   are trailing: a rating change reflects the game before, not the game itself.
2. **What the market prices between the opening and the close is mostly lineup
   and availability news.** A projection that uses each game's projected minutes
   and player ratings has more than twice pace_eff's CLV from the opening (+0.85
   vs +0.39 points). It also has CLV from noon (+0.43 vs −0.02). That CLV sits mostly in
   games where a regular sits out or returns (+0.57 from noon, against +0.29 in
   the other games). The same projector run on the previous game's minutes
   instead of the day's projected minutes drops to +0.14 from noon, and to +0.07
   in lineup-change games.
3. **But this does not show that anyone could have had that information at noon.**
   The pre-registered late-news check fails. The projection still predicts line
   moves that happen in the last two hours before tip (CLV from the ~2 h anchor
   +0.16, edge vs move correlation +0.18), in every season. The archived DnT file
   evidently contains news that reached the market late in the day. Its noon
   CLV is an upper bound on what a noon bettor could have had, not a measurement
   of it. The file's capture time is unknown, and the 2024-25 files carry the most
   same-day news (GATE.md).
4. **No edge.** Against the true close it covers 49.8% (95% Wilson 48.2–51.4%)
   and loses 5.0% at real prices. Given the close, its weight is zero (−0.05, CI
   −0.21 to +0.11). The main pre-registration's edge bar is not met.

## The information bar

Pre-registered: I1, opening CLV lower bound above +0.39 (pace_eff's estimate);
I2, noon CLV lower bound above 0; both must survive the bug-first checks.

| criterion | result | met |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | opening CLV **+0.85** (+0.76 to +0.94), n = 3,220 | yes, numerically |
| I2 | noon CLV **+0.43** (+0.37 to +0.50), n = 3,655 | yes, numerically |
| bug-first check 4 (late news) | CLV from late evening (~2 h) **+0.16** (+0.12 to +0.20); positive in every season | **fails** |

**Verdict: partly supported.** The hypothesis that the repricing is lineup news
is supported (decomposition and placebo below). The claim that a lineup-aware
projection "gets there first" at noon is **not established**, because the input
file carries news from after noon. By the pre-registered rule, neither number
counts as information a noon bettor could have had.

## Provenance of the Dunks & Threes inputs

*Added after the results, at the orchestrating review's request, from Dunks &
Threes' methodology page (October 2026, supplied by the owner). No number in
this file changed and nothing was re-run.*

The page states three things:

- (a) game predictions and projected minutes update with known injuries during
  the day, and within 30 minutes of tip-off they update more often, using
  official player status;
- (b) for 2024-25 and 2025-26 the API returns the predictions published before
  each game at the time; for earlier seasons it returns re-predictions by the
  current model, retrained before each season on earlier seasons only, with
  some design choices made with recent seasons in view;
- (c) Expected EPM never includes games played on that date.

What that means for these results:

1. **The late-news signature is what (a) predicts.** The archived file is the
   final pre-tip version, so it carries injury and lineup news up to about 30
   minutes before tip. That is why the projection still predicts line moves from
   the ~2 h and ~1 h anchors. It also confirms the reading above: the noon CLV
   is an upper bound, not something a noon bettor had.
2. **2022-23 and 2023-24 are upper bounds for a second reason: model vintage.**
   Their inputs are re-predictions by a later model whose design choices saw
   recent seasons. Even with no timing issue, their CLV is not what a bettor
   using DnT at the time would have had. (2021-22, also a re-prediction, is a
   training season only.)
3. **2024-25 is the only test season whose file is what was published
   pre-game.** Its per-season numbers are the only ones from a live-published
   file: opening **+1.08** (+0.92 to +1.24), noon **+0.49** (+0.38 to +0.61),
   late evening **+0.26** (+0.19 to +0.34) (report.md §1b). Its late-evening
   CLV is the largest of the three seasons, which fits a file captured close to
   tip. The gate's 2024-25 flag fits too: more absences anticipated and fewer
   surprise scratches (GATE.md). Even this season is a final pre-tip version, so
   it is an upper bound on what was knowable at noon.
4. **(c) is consistent with gate G2.** The rating change into a game is unrelated
   to that game's performance (0.016) and strongly related to the game before
   (0.456). The page does not say whether the earlier seasons' ratings are also
   re-computed by a later model. If they are, point 2 applies to the placebo and
   to the rating half of lineup_pace_eff as well.

The verdicts do not change: the information bar is partly supported (the
repricing is availability news; "getting there first" at noon is not
established), and there is no edge.

pace_eff's information is gone by noon, and the placebo's is gone by the
afternoon (~5h). The lineup projection keeps predicting the move until about an
hour before tip. A projection made from information available at noon would
not be expected to predict how the line moves between 2 hours and 15 minutes out. One
that includes late injury and lineup news would. The correlation is not affected
by the projection's mean error (below), which would shift every edge by the same
amount.

## Decomposition: lineup-change games

A game is a lineup change if either team has a player whom DnT projects under 5
minutes after he played 25+ in the team's previous game, or 25+ after he played
under 5 (including absent). That covers 654 of 1,219, 625 of 1,222 and 716 of
1,223 games (report.md §5). CLV pooled:

| projector | games | opening | noon | afternoon | near close |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lineup_pace_eff | lineup change | +1.04 (+0.90 to +1.18) | **+0.57** (+0.47 to +0.67) | +0.30 (+0.23 to +0.36) | +0.07 (+0.03 to +0.10) |
| lineup_pace_eff | no change | +0.66 (+0.54 to +0.78) | +0.29 (+0.21 to +0.37) | +0.09 (+0.04 to +0.14) | +0.01 (−0.02 to +0.05) |
| placebo | lineup change | +0.46 (+0.31 to +0.60) | +0.07 (−0.03 to +0.17) | −0.01 (−0.08 to +0.06) | −0.01 (−0.04 to +0.03) |
| placebo | no change | +0.63 (+0.51 to +0.76) | +0.22 (+0.14 to +0.30) | +0.07 (+0.01 to +0.12) | +0.00 (−0.03 to +0.03) |
| pace_eff | lineup change | +0.48 (+0.33 to +0.63) | −0.04 (−0.14 to +0.07) | −0.00 (−0.07 to +0.07) | −0.01 (−0.05 to +0.03) |
| pace_eff | no change | +0.30 (+0.17 to +0.42) | +0.01 (−0.07 to +0.09) | +0.05 (−0.01 to +0.10) | +0.04 (+0.01 to +0.07) |

As pre-registered, noon CLV is larger in lineup-change games. The day's
projected minutes are what carry it: swapping them for the previous game's
minutes takes lineup-change noon CLV from +0.57 to +0.07. In no-change games,
ratings with ordinary minutes (the placebo) already reach +0.22 from noon.

The placebo uses no same-day minutes: each player's actual minutes in his team's
previous game replace DnT's on 97.6% of rows (each team's first game of a
season and unmatched players keep DnT's). Its rows dated D are the ratings, which
G2 shows are trailing. Its late-evening CLV is +0.03 (−0.01 to +0.06), so it
shows no late-news signature. It is an unregistered diagnostic. Its noon CLV of
+0.14 (+0.07 to +0.20) matches elo_total's +0.14, and like that number it would
need its own pre-registered test.

**These early-anchor Pinnacle cells look like an edge. By the pre-registration
they are a bug until shown otherwise, and they have a known cause.** They are what
CLV of 0.4 to 0.9 points does to a bet struck at the earlier line. The CLV
itself comes from a file that carries news from late in the game day (late-news
check above). Betting at noon on information that arrived at 5 pm is hindsight,
not an edge. At Pinnacle's actual price neither interval excludes zero, and none
of these cells is the primary comparison. Against each anchor's consensus the
picture is the same and smaller: opening 52.0% (50.3–53.8%), real ROI −0.8%;
noon 51.7% (50.1–53.3%), −1.4% (§3b). A real test needs projections captured with
timestamps before the line they are bet against. That means live collection from
2025-26 on, with DnT pulled at fixed times and stored with its poll time.

## Blend regression and accuracy

`actual = a + b·true close + c·lineup_pace_eff`, pooled tuned: b = 1.029 (0.886,
1.171), **c = −0.049 (−0.211, +0.114)**. With both models: close +1.084,
pace_eff −0.115 (−0.271, +0.041), lineup −0.007 (−0.179, +0.166). By the time of
the close the market has all of it.

MAE against the actual total (pooled): lineup_pace_eff 14.54 (untuned defaults
14.46), true close 14.16, pace_eff 14.70. RMSE 18.34 against pace_eff's 18.49.
Mean error by season −2.70, +1.08, −0.14 (pace_eff −0.06, +1.28, +0.68). The fixed
+2 constant leaves 2022-23 low (report.md §7).

Walk-forward fit (scale, shrinkage): 1.25/5 for 2022-23, 1.0/5 for 2023-24,
1.0/0 for 2024-25. Tuning did not help out of sample: test RMSE was 18.69, 18.30
and 18.00 tuned against 18.65, 18.10 and 18.00 with the defaults.

## Bug-first checks

1. **Gate and truncation.** G1 and G2 pass (GATE.md). Every registered
   projector, including `lineup_pace_eff`, passes the truncation test
   (`tests/test_leakage.py`). The external-file test (`tests/test_lineup.py`)
   deletes every DnT and rating file dated after D, and every other game's rows
   dated D. The projection is bit-identical in both untuned and tuned states. A
   leaky reference mean is caught (positive control).
2. **Join.** DnT games matched to legacy regular-season games: 1,211 of 1,230,
   1,233 of 1,236, 1,237 of 1,237, 1,237 of 1,237. Games with no DnT lineup for a
   team: 19 to 22 per training set (fit table); in the test seasons, 3 games in 2022-23
   (the 3 unjoined games) and none after. They get zero lineup deviations.
3. **Placebo.** Same-day minutes replaced by previous-game minutes: noon CLV
   falls from +0.43 to +0.14 and late-evening CLV from +0.16 to +0.03. The CLV
   comes from the day's minutes, as the hypothesis says.
4. **Late-news signature. Fails** (see above). CLV from the late-evening anchor is
   +0.12, +0.11 and +0.26 by season, and from near close +0.04 pooled. The noon
   CLV cannot be credited to information available at noon.
5. **Drift.** CLV is positive on both sides: overs +0.81 / +0.39 and unders
   +0.89 / +0.47 (opening / noon). Not a market drift.
6. **Season stability.** Paired CLV over pace_eff is positive in every season
   at the opening and at noon (§1c).

## What this means for the main result

pace_eff's +0.39 from the opening was stale box-score information. This
experiment says what the market adds between the opening and the close: mostly
who plays. Ratings with ordinary minutes (the placebo, and elo_total) still show
some CLV from noon (+0.14) and none from the afternoon. Anything beyond that in
this experiment depends on a file of unknown capture time, so it cannot be
claimed. Nothing here is an edge. A clean test of "can a lineup projection beat
the noon line" needs DnT projections captured live at a known time before noon,
from 2025-26 onwards, with this pre-registration's bars.
